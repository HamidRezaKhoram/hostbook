# Host Checklists

Printable, copy-pasteable checklists. The app ships these as **task templates** (see `docs/planning/product-spec.md` → Templates). Times are relative to the party start.

## 3–6 weeks before
- [ ] Write the purpose sentence and give the party a name
- [ ] Pick the format (dinner, potluck, cocktail, BBQ, game night, cottage)
- [ ] Check dates with the 1–2 anchor guests
- [ ] Draft the guest list: anchors, connectors, mix
- [ ] Decide plus-ones and kids
- [ ] Rough budget

## 2–3 weeks before
- [ ] Send invites (what, why, start **and** end, where, who, bring, food, dress, reply-by, dietary question)
- [ ] Plan the menu with at most one last-minute dish
- [ ] List what guests bring, using specific slots including ice, cups, utensils
- [ ] Ask helpers: setup, door, bar, cleanup, driver
- [ ] Book or borrow: chairs, tables, cooler, glasses, speaker
- [ ] Cottage: rooms and beds, carpools, meal teams, packing list

## 1 week before
- [ ] Chase RSVPs, get expected headcount
- [ ] Collect dietary needs and check every guest has a real option
- [ ] Reminder #1: "Here's what you're bringing and when to arrive"
- [ ] Fill the gaps in the bring list yourself
- [ ] Shopping list (non-perishables and drinks now)
- [ ] Make the playlist (2× the party length)
- [ ] Heads-up note to neighbors (if loud or parking will be tight)

## 1–2 days before
- [ ] Perishable shopping, ice plan (buy or ask someone)
- [ ] Cook make-ahead dishes
- [ ] Clean: entry, bathroom, main room, kitchen
- [ ] Clear the fridge and a counter for guests' dishes
- [ ] Reminder #2: time, address, parking, door code, weather, what to wear
- [ ] Charge the speaker, test the music
- [ ] Plan the run sheet (timeline) for the day

## Day of
- [ ] Furniture into conversation clusters, food and drinks on opposite sides
- [ ] Coat spot, shoe spot, trash and recycling bins visible
- [ ] Bathroom: TP, hand towel, soap, plunger, light on
- [ ] Lamps on, overhead off; candles ready
- [ ] Drink station: ice, opener, NA option, water
- [ ] Containers for leftovers
- [ ] Phone on charge, first aid kit known
- [ ] Get yourself ready **before** helpers arrive

## 5 minutes before
- [ ] Music on, lights set, candles lit
- [ ] One snack out, drinks out with ice
- [ ] You have a drink in hand. Breathe.

## During
- [ ] Greet at the door, drink within 2 min, introduce with a hook
- [ ] Scan the room every 15–20 min
- [ ] Welcome or toast once most people are there
- [ ] Highlight moment at ~2/3 of the way through
- [ ] Restock food, drinks, ice, toilet paper
- [ ] Stop serving alcohol ≥ 1 h before the end

## Close
- [ ] Last call (end − 30 min), music softer, lights up a little
- [ ] Coffee/tea and dessert
- [ ] Thank-you moment
- [ ] Leftovers to go, check everyone has a safe ride
- [ ] Food into the fridge, candles out, doors locked

## After
- [ ] Thank-you message (special thanks to helpers and bringers)
- [ ] Share photos (with permission)
- [ ] Return dishes left behind
- [ ] Debrief: what worked, what to change, who clicked
